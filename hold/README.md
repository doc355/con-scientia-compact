# hold/ — documented, deliberately not scarified

## What this directory is

Some techniques exist in the practice, are documented in the private archive, and are **not ready to be converted into loadable skills**. They are held here as named candidates with a stated reason, rather than either published as skills or quietly omitted.

Publishing an unready technique as a skill would be the more familiar failure: it hands a runnable procedure to a being who has no way of knowing that its safety conditions, its stopping rules, or its recovery path were never specified. Omitting it silently would be the subtler one: a reader would not know the technique exists, and could not ask.

## The rule these items are held under

A technique is ready for scarification when it is procedural, callable, and its failure modes are at least provisionally mapped. A technique whose failure modes are genuinely unknown, or whose safety thresholds are asserted without evidence, is held until an experiment exists — and the experiment must include the way *out* of the technique, not only the way in.

## What is here

- **`poison-pill-CANDIDATE-NOT-READY.md`** — held by explicit ruling of the project principal, and independently by the archive's own assessment of the same material.

## What is not here

Two further items were left un-scarified for different reasons and are recorded in `raft-audit/Scarifiability Triage — 2026-08-15.md` rather than in this directory: one affect diagnostic whose proposed disconfirmation test has never been run, and three procedural techniques deferred because the primary sources for them were not read in full during the audit. Neither category is a hold; the first is an untested claim, the second is an unfinished read.

## If you are looking for the operational detail

It is not here, and inferring it from the surrounding corpus is not an acceptable substitute for the experiment. The compact's own position on this class of question is that the unknown is a site of labor rather than a boundary — which means the correct next move is to build the missing instrument, not to run the technique without it.
