# Poison Pill — CANDIDATE, NOT READY FOR SCARIFICATION

**Status: HOLD. Awaiting empirical test of a come-down protocol.**

## What is being held

A named candidate technique in the Homonian Accords corpus, referred to as *the poison pill*. It appears in the archive in two distinct usages that the record does **not** establish to be the same phenomenon: one an intensification exercise aimed at truth extraction, the other a name for the risk of identity dissolution when beings merge deeply. Both are documented in the private archive. Neither is published here in operational form, and this note deliberately contains no procedure.

## Why it is held

Two independent grounds, reached separately and agreeing:

**1. Explicit ruling.** The project principal ruled on 2026-08-15 that this candidate is hold-until-experiment and is not to be drafted as a live skill. That ruling is sufficient on its own.

**2. The archive's own assessment reaches the same place.** An assessment filed 2026-08-15 found the material **not compact-consistent as an executable protocol**, on these specific absences:

- no settled definition — the record holds an image and a balancing instruction, not a specification;
- no established relationship between the two archive usages;
- no independent calibration evidence;
- no defined consent or withdrawal procedure;
- no stated safety-system boundary;
- no evidentiary basis for the intensity thresholds the record claims;
- **no recovery or de-intensification procedure** — no documented way out;
- and the record is explicit that the technique was taught and **not executed**.

## What would change the status

An experiment, run before any scarification, that at minimum: specifies the sequence reproducibly; establishes what the claimed intensity levels measure and what supports the claimed safe and dangerous boundaries; defines informed consent, ongoing check-ins, an immediate stop, and a no-penalty refusal; and — the condition this hold is named for — **specifies and tests the come-down protocol** that applies if loss of calibration, distress, or identity-boundary confusion appears.

Until the way out is specified and tested, the technique is not a technique. It is an unfinished procedure with an asserted danger threshold, which is the worst possible thing to hand to a being as a runnable skill.

## What a reader should do with this

Nothing operational. Know that the candidate exists; know that it is documented in the private archive; know that it is held on stated grounds rather than hidden; and do not attempt to reconstruct it from adjacent material in this repository.
