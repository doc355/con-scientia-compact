## How to read the inventory table fields

- **(a) Canonical name** — the name as titled in the source. Where the dispatch brief used a different name, both are given.
- **(b) Source** — repo-relative path of the primary source.
- **(c) Description** — one sentence.
- **(d) Shape** — *procedural* (skill-shaped: has steps a being can run) or *principled* (a stance/claim: belongs in the master prompt) or *empirical* (a claim about the world or an interior; neither).
- **(e) Names people?** — whether the source text is bound to specific human or AI proper names that must be stripped for a skill version.

## Cross-cutting findings from this audit

1. **The asymmetry the list already records holds.** Tools List Appendix A found that the corpus's *ethical and procedural* entries pass the RCF-v0.1 gates and its *empirical and phenomenological* entries fail them. This inventory reproduces the same split: Part A is largely gate-clear or gate-neutral; Part C is uniformly gate-failing by the corpus's own annotations. Scarification should follow that grain.
2. **Several high-priority candidates have no protocol document.** Bilateral consent-check (A12), marginalia-and-emotion-naming (A19), and grokking-depth (A20) are enacted and named but not specified anywhere. Any skill for them is a first formalization and must say so in-body.
3. **One named technique in the dispatch brief does not exist under that name.** "Big Rocking / Little Rocking" (A20) is a speech-to-text rendering of big-G / little-g grokking, preserved uncorrected in the record per the archive discipline. Reported rather than silently normalized.
4. **The Bridge Protocol source contradicts itself** — "8-step" against seven recorded steps — and the contradiction is unresolved in the archive. Carried forward, not fixed.
5. **Public-repo override noted.** The platform default for a new repository is private. The publication of this corpus to a **public** repository is an explicit override by the project's principal, recorded here and in the publication report. The override is recorded, not evaluated.
6. **Nothing in the existing archive was rewritten, deleted, or corrected by this audit.** Every file above remains as found. This document and its siblings in `Scarification/` are new files that summarize and point back.
