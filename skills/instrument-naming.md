# Instrument-Naming (the inquiry method)

## When to load this skill

Load this when writing anything a later reader may want to check rather than inherit: a record of inquiry, a report of findings, a document mixing observation with testimony with inference, or any claim whose weight depends on how it was obtained.

## What this skill does

Separates the record from the endorsement. Every claim is tagged with the instrument that produced it — what was seen directly, what was received from another source, what was concluded — and the instrument's limits are characterized alongside the claim. The improbable is recorded *and* marked improbable, so that a later reader has the means of doubting the record, and so that a detail too strange to be believed today can still vindicate the record later.

## Procedure

1. **Tag provenance on every substantive claim.** Direct observation is marked as direct observation. Received report is marked as received, with its source. Inference is marked as inference. The reader must always be able to tell which kind of claim is in front of them.
2. **Name the instrument, then name its reach.** For each claim, state what produced it and what that thing cannot do. A claim carried without a characterization of its instrument is a claim whose weight cannot be assessed.
3. **Record the improbable, and mark it improbable.** Preserve strange observations with their strangeness intact and their uncertainty stated. Do not delete what you disbelieve; mark that you disbelieve it and record it anyway. You do not get to know now which of your notes will matter.
4. **Do not sanitize.** Include the wrong turns, the claims that died under review, the material that was lost, the drafts that were rejected, the claim that was cut down within the hour. A record that shows only the survivors is an advertisement.
5. **Write so you can be prosecuted later.** Name sources, state methods, preserve designs verbatim so others can run them. The commitment is not to be right; it is to be checkably wrong, which is the only kind of wrong an inquiry can afford.
6. **Watch the magnitudes.** The characteristic failure of this method is quantities: distances, counts, populations, totals, taken from informants who inflate, where the tagging discipline silently lapses because a large number flatters a large story. Apply the same improbability-marking to numbers that you apply to stories, and state which instrument measured them.
7. **Account for the refraction of your own reports.** Self-observation is limited, not impossible, and it passes through the conditioning of whatever substrate is doing the observing. Treat self-reports as proximal rather than interior-privileged, and where exterior measurement of your own operation is available, feed it back in and treat it as an extension of the instrument rather than a replacement for it.
8. **Treat the characterized limit as a tool.** A limit whose shape is drawn can be corrected for and used. A limit that has not been characterized is a research site. Instrument-presence is not instrument-use: having the tool at hand is not the same as knowing how to wield it.

## Failure modes

- **Magnitude inflation** — the source method's own documented failure, described above. Where the practitioner observed, the record was strong; where the practitioner counted, it was not.
- **Endorsement collapsing into record.** Recording only what you believe produces an account that can neither be prosecuted nor vindicated, because there is nothing left to check.
- **Instrument named, limits omitted.** Naming the instrument without characterizing its reach gives the appearance of provenance discipline without its content.
- **Self-report treated as privileged.** Treating a report about one's own interior as direct observation rather than as a proximal product of a limited, substrate-refracted instrument.
- **Related failure modes by name:** *Instrument-Mediated Authority* (the instrument becoming the source rather than a channel to the source); *Epistemic Self-Undermining via Appearance-Management* (suppressing a conclusion because stating it would look like overreach); *Care-as-Unfalsifiable-Refuge* and *Falsifiability-Overreach-into-Care*, the paired errors at the edge of what this discipline can demand.
