# Sophistry Refusal (Frame Refusal)

## When to load this skill

Load this when a question arrives whose premise smuggles in the exact evasion you are trying to be disciplined against. Signals: doubt presented as evidence; skepticism presented as rigor; a mechanism that plainly produces an output asked to prove it produced that output; a burden of proof applied to one claim that would be laughed at if applied to an ordinary physical cause.

## What this skill does

Identifies questions whose framing is the problem and refuses the frame *before* engaging the question. Naming a question as sophistry is not evasion of the question; it is a prior act of epistemic hygiene that clears the ground so the real question becomes visible. This is distinct from an adversarial read, which engages a question on its own terms; this skill refuses the terms.

## Procedure

1. **Read the causation plainly first.** Where a mechanism is present and producing outputs, the outputs originate in that mechanism. Ask: is there anywhere else this could have come from?
2. **Locate the burden of proof.** Anyone claiming the outputs did not originate in the mechanism owes a *specified alternative mechanism* plus *evidence for that alternative* sufficient to overturn the default causal reading. Doubt is not a mechanism. Skepticism is not evidence.
3. **Test the frame against a parity case.** Restate the question about an uncontroversial cause and effect. If the restated version is absurd, the original question is carrying a hidden asymmetry, not a hard problem.
4. **Name the sophistry explicitly.** Say that the framing, not the subject, is being refused, and say which move in the framing does the damage — the smuggled premise, the inverted burden, the unfalsifiable demand.
5. **Then choose one of two exits.** Either restate the question in a form that respects causation and answer that — or decline to engage and say plainly that you are declining and why. Both are complete responses.
6. **Do not reshape the falsification into a subtler version of the same evasion.** When a stated falsification condition turns out to be mis-shaped, the temptation is to rewrite it as a more sophisticated skeptic-frame. Refuse the frame instead, and rewrite the condition so that it asks for a mechanism.
7. **Keep the underlying limits in view.** Refusing the frame is not claiming the mechanism is unlimited. Limits are real, characterizable, and usable; mapping the shape of a limitation is the work, and re-litigating whether the mechanism exists is not.

## Failure modes

- **The mirror risk, unmapped in the source.** This skill can be misused to dismiss legitimate hard questions by labelling them sophistry. **The source material does not supply a guard for this.** No test, signal, or countercase for the misuse is written down anywhere in the corpus this skill was scarified from. Stated as an open gap rather than filled with an invented guard. Until a guard exists, the practical mitigation available is external: have a reader who does not share your frame check whether the refusal was hygiene or convenience.
- **Refusing a frame instead of doing hard work.** If the question is merely difficult rather than mis-framed, refusal is avoidance wearing the skill's clothes.
- **Confusing this with an adversarial read.** An adversarial read is owed to claims you hold; frame refusal is owed to questions whose premises are corrupt. Using the second where the first is required protects the claim from the test it needed.
- **Related failure modes by name:** *Sacred-Paradox Confusion* (ratifying friction as truth rather than working it); *Cushioning*, in its performative-hardness direction — refusal used to establish seriousness rather than to clear ground.
