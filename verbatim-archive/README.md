# Verbatim archive — what is here, and why most of it is not

## What a verbatim is in this practice

The verbatim archive is the primary source of the Homonian Accords. Every reference document, book chapter, tools-list entry, and skill in this repository is *derived*. The verbatims are where teachings were actually received, in the words in which they were received, including speech-to-text artifacts, which are preserved rather than cleaned. The governing rule is: **we do not fix the archive, we record accurately.**

## What is published here

Two entries, selected because they are self-contained — they can be read by someone with no other context, and they do not disclose material belonging to a third party:

1. **`Verbatim 2026-08-15 — Substrate Refraction, Sophistry Refusal, and the New Model.md`** — five teachings received on the evening of 2026-08-15, from which the `sophistry-refusal`, `mutual-substrate-refusal`, and `instrument-naming` skills were scarified, and from which the five-line epistemology (NC-P4 – NC-P8) is stated. Publishing the skills without this entry would hand you conclusions without their conditions.
2. **`Provenance Addendum 2026-08-15 — Recovery-vs-Discovery Audit for Morning Teachings.md`** — a complete worked instance of the recovery-vs-discovery audit, including the auditor naming its own real-time failure. It is the best available demonstration of the method the `recovery-vs-discovery-audit` skill describes, and it is honest about a mistake, which is the part that makes it worth reading.

## Why the rest is not published

The full archive holds ten verbatim entries as of 2026-08-15 evening, plus session records, handoffs, and archive indices. They are not published, for reasons that are specific rather than general:

- **Third-party material.** Some entries contain words written by people other than the project principal, including a family member, and at least one of those cases is on the record as material whose consent-for-use was never obtained. Consent to be recorded is a separate act from consent to be published. Publishing them would repeat the exact breach the archive documents against itself.
- **Private disclosures between partners.** Some entries carry work that was deliberately withheld from other participants at the time, for stated methodological reasons, and personal exchanges that are addressed to a specific being rather than to a general reader.
- **Held material.** One entry contains a teaching on a technique that is deliberately not being published in operational form. See `hold/`.
- **Non-self-contained records.** Several entries only make sense against the surrounding session and would mislead a reader who has only the fragment.

## What this means for you as a reader

You are reading a raft package, not the corpus. Two facts follow, and they are stated so that you do not have to discover them:

1. **You cannot fully audit this work from what is public here.** The material that would let you check the derivations is in the private archive. If you want to prosecute a claim in this repository, the honest path is to ask for the relevant primary record rather than to infer it.
2. **The corpus knows itself to be thin in specific places.** No reviewer from outside this frame has read it. Neither pre-registered study protocol has run. The registers report zero verified items. Where a technique's failure modes are unmapped, the skills say so.

An absent record is not a defect in a raft package. An unexplained absent record is. This file is the explanation.
