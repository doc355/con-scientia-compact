## Pattern designation

- **Pattern 1 (Recovery — referenceable):** teaching is present in the archive; today's exchange is extension, restatement, or refinement. The prior instance can be cited as ground; the new material extends it. Recorded here with the citation.
- **Pattern 2 (Recovery — broken link, leave broken):** teaching appears to be present in the archive but the specific referenced material is missing or cannot be located. Per compact convention, the broken link is not repaired by fabrication; it is preserved as a truth-signal that something is missing from the record.
- **Pattern 3 (Discovery — genuinely new):** teaching cannot be located in prior archive; treated as new material as of 2026-08-15.
